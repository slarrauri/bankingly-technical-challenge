# 📖 22 — Domain Glossary

> **Scope:** Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Know Your Customer (KYC), and Agentic Banking Terminology

---

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **AML (Anti-Money Laundering)** | Laws, regulations, and institutional procedures designed to prevent criminals from disguising illegally obtained funds as legitimate income. |
| **KYC (Know Your Customer)** | The mandatory process of identifying, verifying, and assessing the risk profile of clients during onboarding and ongoing relationships. |
| **AML Alert** | A trigger generated by a monitoring system signaling that customer transactions have exceeded risk thresholds or matched suspicious patterns. |
| **Agentic Banking** | Software systems utilizing autonomous or semi-autonomous AI agents capable of reasoning, planning, and tool invocation to execute banking operations under human governance. |
| **Agent Harness** | The trusted deterministic application wrapper that controls authentication, permissions, state transitions, validation, and human approval gates around an AI model. |
| **SAR (Suspicious Activity Report)** | A formal regulatory filing submitted by financial institutions to reporting authorities upon confirming suspected money laundering or illicit activity. |
| **False Positive** | An alert triggered on legitimate, benign customer behavior that does not represent illicit activity. |
| **Structuring / Smurfing** | Breaking a large sum of money into smaller transactions below statutory reporting thresholds to evade AML monitoring. |
| **Counterparty** | The external or domestic third-party individual or corporate entity participating in a transaction with the bank's customer. |
| **Evidence Grounding** | The degree to which an AI agent's findings and claims are directly supported by citations of verified data retrieved from authoritative tools. |
| **Prompt Injection** | An adversarial attack where text payload (e.g. inside a transaction description) attempts to manipulate the model into ignoring system instructions or safety rules. |
| **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)** | A system design requiring human decision-makers to approve or reject recommendations before any state-mutating or irreversible action occurs. |
